# Disclaimer

**Read this before acting on anything in this repository.**

## This is an index, not advice

This repository is an informational catalogue of software, models, datasets and
related resources for artificial intelligence in pathology. It is **not** medical
advice, **not** a clinical guideline, and **not** a recommendation to use any
particular tool on patient material.

## Inclusion is not endorsement

A resource appears here because it exists, is publicly accessible, and can be
described honestly. That is all. There are no rankings, no scores, and no
"best of" claims. Ordering within a section is alphabetical or chronological.

## Regulatory status is reported, not certified

Where an entry records a regulatory status, that status is transcribed **from the
regulator's own public record**, with a link and the date it was checked. It is
never taken from vendor marketing.

Even so:

- **Clearance in one jurisdiction says nothing about another.** An FDA clearance
  is not a CE mark, is not a UKCA mark, and is not a CDSCO licence.
- **Clearance is specific to a stated indication for use.** A device cleared for
  one task on one specimen type is not cleared for anything else.
- **Status changes.** Every claim carries a `verified_on` date. If that date is
  old, treat the claim as unverified and check the primary source yourself.
- **Anything marked "research use only" must not be used for primary diagnosis**
  or to direct patient management.

## Validation remains your responsibility

Performance reported in a paper, on a leaderboard, or by a vendor does not
transfer to your laboratory. Scanner, stain protocol, patient population and
preparation all shift results. You and your institution remain responsible for
local validation, for regulatory compliance in your own jurisdiction, and for
every clinical decision made.

## Data protection

Some catalogued tools transmit images to a third-party server. Entries record
this in the `sends_data_offsite` field and surface it as a warning, but that flag
is a convenience, not a guarantee. **Before uploading any patient material
anywhere, confirm the data flow yourself** and satisfy your obligations under the
law that applies to you — India's DPDP Act, the GDPR, HIPAA, or otherwise.
